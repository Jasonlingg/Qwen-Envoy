# W4: For paper QA, whole long paper or iterative selected-passage search/inspection?

Research window (UTC): 2026-10-26T20:33:27Z to 2026-10-26T20:35:29Z. The common start is the pilot start; all five questions were in scope throughout.

Recommendation: Keep iterative search and inspection of selected passages as the default paper-reading path, with a bounded ability to inspect neighboring sections and revisit search; test whole-paper input as a matched comparator on papers that fit context.

## Source-linked claims

- Long-context model performance can degrade when relevant information sits in the middle of a long input. [Abstract, lines 16-18](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172)
- A QASPER document-QA system explicitly separated text extraction, evidence retrieval, and answer generation and reported a 7.19 Answer-F1 gain over its baselines. [Abstract, lines 16-19](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01959)
- LongRAG argues that passage chunking can lose global context and noisy retrieval can obscure evidence, motivating some access to broader structure. [Abstract, lines 15-18](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18050)

Baseline: One whole-paper prompt with the same model, decoding, question, source text normalization, and maximum total input/output token cap wherever the paper fits.

Cheapest matched test: Replay the same answerable and unanswerable questions through whole-paper input and the current iterative search/read/extract path. For papers exceeding the limit, report a separate infeasible stratum rather than truncating silently. Blind-review atomic answer support, completeness, tool failures, tokens, and latency.

Decision rule: Retain iterative inspection if it yields at least 3 more supported answers on 20 answerable cases, or equal support with at least 25% fewer total input tokens, and does not add more than 1 execution-error episode on 40. Whole-paper wins only if it improves supported answers by at least 3 and fits at least 90% of the selected paper set within the configured context cap. These are pilot thresholds.

Uncertainty: The studies are not a direct Qwen3-8B code-reader comparison. Retrieval misses, multi-section synthesis, and paper-length distribution may overturn the default; local token and latency measurements are unset.
