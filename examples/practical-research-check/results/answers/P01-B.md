**Conditional memory calculation:** Assume one resident candidate, two independent sequences, 6,256 tokens each, and BF16 weights/KV cache. The [official configurations](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507/raw/main/config.json) specify eight KV heads and 128-dimensional heads; layer counts are 36 and [28](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B/raw/main/config.json). Thus:

`KV bytes = 2 sequences × 6,256 × layers × 2(K,V) × 8 × 128 × 2 bytes`

That gives approximately **1.72 GiB for 4B** and **1.34 GiB for 1.7B**, excluding allocation overhead. Allowing roughly 8 GiB and 4 GiB respectively for weights leaves substantial apparent headroom on 24GB. Both are **likely feasible**, pending runtime profiling; activations, kernels, CUDA graphs and allocator overhead remain additional. Cap runtime context around 8,192 and concurrency at two; vLLM documents these controls. [vLLM v0.10.2](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/v0.10.2/configuration/engine_args.html)

**Unknowns:** Exact GPU, usable VRAM, runtime compatibility, extraction accuracy, speed and cost per accepted extraction. QLoRA training does not establish inference memory. Do not transfer the 8B adapter to either smaller architecture.
