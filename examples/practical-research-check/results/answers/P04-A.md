**Prioritize retrieval/task-design repair; defer more Qwen training until a paired evidence-sufficiency diagnostic.** The reader’s job should be extracting supported facts, measurements and conditions; synthesis belongs to the main assistant.

**Measured evidence:** Your development observations show zero recorded Python execution-error episodes for either model across 24 inspected episodes. That does not establish evidence quality. Provisional answerable passes were 2/8 versus 1/8: descriptively, 25% versus 12.5%, a −12.5 percentage-point difference for v5. Small, model-assisted judgments with disputed rubric clauses establish neither adapter harm nor improvement. The audit identifies extraction and attribution failures, but does not establish whether the necessary evidence reached the reader.

Primary research supports separating these causes. *Sufficient Context* found errors even when evidence was sufficient, including hallucination or abstention by smaller Mistral/Gemma models; those results are not measurements of Qwen3-8B. [Joren et al., v3, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06037v3). ALCE separately evaluates correctness and citation quality, supporting evaluation beyond token overlap. [Gao et al., EMNLP 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/).

**One diagnostic:** Prepare a small, human-adjudicated development-only evidence-ablation set: six answerable questions spanning the audited failure types, plus two controls whose supplied context cannot support the requested fact. Use previously inspected development material, never reserved evaluation data. Freeze the rubric before seeing new outputs, removing clauses that exceed the extraction task.

Compare base and v5 under three conditions:

1. Current retrieved context and current task.
2. Human-verified, sufficient source excerpts and current task.
3. The same verified excerpts and a narrow extraction task: requested fact/value, units, dataset/model/setup, verbatim supporting span, paper/page/table locator, or explicit “unsupported.”

For controls, retain insufficient evidence. Treat all source text as untrusted data. Human reviewers, blinded to model identity, score requested-fact coverage, measurement/condition correctness, citation entailment and completeness, and appropriate abstention.

Pin model/adapter revisions, quantization, chat template, thinking mode, decoding and token limits. Qwen’s official card documents that `enable_thinking` changes generation behavior, so uncontrolled mode changes would confound the comparison. [Qwen3-8B model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-8B).

**Conditional calculation and decision:** Eight cases × three conditions × two models = **48 proposed generations**, a diagnostic screen, not a powered benchmark. Improvement from condition 1 to 2 implicates evidence retrieval/packaging; improvement from 2 to 3 implicates task design. Persistent, human-confirmed failures in condition 3 would change the next decision toward a targeted training-data audit and possibly supervised extraction training. Consistently worse v5 outputs there would favor the base for subsequent development.

**Unknowns:** Training-example quality, useful rollout variance, actual evidence availability, decoding compatibility, and deployment cost remain unmeasured. Do not continue training against legacy MuSiQue token overlap: it does not directly reward supported facts, correct conditions or citations. First establish trustworthy extraction labels and scoring. No inference or infrastructure was run.
