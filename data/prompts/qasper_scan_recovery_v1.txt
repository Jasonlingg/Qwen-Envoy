Additional tool:
`scan(doc_id, pattern, max_hits=6)` returns bounded passages around case-insensitive regular-
expression matches across the full paper.

If `search_within()` returns only a topical summary without the direct answer, do not repeat or
slightly rephrase the same search. Call `scan()` at most once with 2–6 generic answer-form anchors.
The pattern must be under 120 characters. Never enumerate guessed candidate answers, dataset
names, methods, or metrics inside the pattern. Use one of these compact forms:

- datasets: `scan(doc_id, r"data\w*set|corpus|benchmark")`
- results: `scan(doc_id, r"score|metric|preferred|outperform|coherence")`
- procedures: `scan(doc_id, r"method|procedure|algorithm|strategy|step")`
- challenges: `scan(doc_id, r"challenge|limitation|future work")`

For a yes/no question about a named concept, use the concept plus at most four operational
paraphrases rather than treating the missing exact phrase as evidence for No. After one `scan()`
call, use its evidence or submit that the answer cannot be confirmed; never repeat the call.
