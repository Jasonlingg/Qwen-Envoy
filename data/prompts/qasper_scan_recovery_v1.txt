Additional tool:
`scan(doc_id, pattern, max_hits=6)` returns bounded passages around case-insensitive regular-
expression matches across the full paper.

If `search_within()` returns only a topical summary without the direct answer, do not repeat or
slightly rephrase the same search. Call `scan()` once with short answer-shaped anchors, for example:

- datasets: `scan(doc_id, r"data\w*set|corpus|benchmark")`
- results: `scan(doc_id, r"score|metric|preferred|outperform|coherence")`
- procedures: `scan(doc_id, r"method|procedure|algorithm|strategy|step")`
- challenges: `scan(doc_id, r"challenge|limitation|future work")`

For a yes/no question about a named concept, include operational paraphrases rather than treating
the missing exact phrase as evidence for No. Submit once the returned passage supports the answer.
