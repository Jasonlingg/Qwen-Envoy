# Rectangle homework attempt, September 1

The exercise asks for the largest area of a rectangle with perimeter 40 units. I let one side be x and wrote the other side as 20 minus x, so the area expression was A(x) = x(20 - x). My first handwritten solution then mistakenly used the derivative 20 - 4x, leading me to x = 5. That value was my attempted answer, not a verified optimum. Substituting x = 5 gives sides 5 and 15, with area 75 square units.

I noticed the area at x = 10 would be 100 square units, so my answer could not be the maximum. I marked the work for review rather than copying the first result into a study guide. The September 3 correction identifies the differentiation mistake and supersedes this attempted answer.
