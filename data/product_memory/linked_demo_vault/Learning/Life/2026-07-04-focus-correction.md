# Correction: starting friction, July 4

I revisited the June phone-drawer idea after four library sessions. On two days I hid the phone but still lost time deciding what to do. On the other two days I wrote a single next action before the session, such as “solve question 3 without notes,” and began sooner even though the phone stayed on the desk. These are informal observations with several changing conditions; they do not prove that next-action planning caused the improvement.

My corrected working belief is that an unclear first task may have mattered more than access to the phone in this small set of sessions. I no longer describe the drawer as the complete solution. I want to combine a written next action with reduced notifications, then compare several sessions using the same start-time measure. The older note is superseded as advice but should remain readable as the original attempt.
