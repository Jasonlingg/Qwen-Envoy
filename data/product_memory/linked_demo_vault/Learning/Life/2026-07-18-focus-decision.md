# Next focus experiment, July 18

For the next two weeks, I will write the first concrete action on a card before each morning work block, silence notifications for 30 minutes, and log the minutes from sitting down to starting that action. I will compare those logs with my previous sessions, while treating the comparison as informal because task difficulty and location may differ.

This is a decision and a measurement plan, not a completed experiment. I have not recorded the two-week results here. If the logs show little change or the routine is too annoying to keep, I will stop recommending it as my default. This decision draws on the July 4 correction, which superseded my June phone-only explanation.
