# Rectangle correction, September 3

I expanded the area expression as 20x - x². Its derivative is 20 - 2x, not 20 - 4x. Setting 20 - 2x to zero gives x = 10; the other side is also 10, and the area is 100 square units. The second derivative is -2, so this critical point is a maximum on the feasible interval from 0 to 20.

The September 1 value of x = 5 came from an arithmetic error in differentiation. It was an attempt, not a second valid method. My useful lesson is to expand the product carefully, check the derivative term by term, and substitute the answer back into the original area expression. This note corrects the earlier answer; it does not show that my later homework scores improved.
